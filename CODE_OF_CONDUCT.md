# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our Pledge

We want participation in Smewlet to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, background, experience level, identity, or opinion.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, especially when giving feedback.
- Assume good faith and stay on topic.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Respect that this is a maintainer-run hobby project with limited time.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Spam or promotion unrelated to the project
- Requests for or discussion of piracy or copyrighted content. Smewlet is a
  management tool and does not support or condone infringement.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues, and pull requests, and may
temporarily or permanently block anyone who violates this code of conduct.

## Reporting

To report a violation, contact the maintainer privately through the GitHub
profile [@Reimaris](https://github.com/Reimaris). Reports are handled
confidentially.
